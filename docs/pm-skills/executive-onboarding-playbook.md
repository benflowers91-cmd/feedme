# Executive Onboarding Playbook

**Type:** Workflow
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/executive-onboarding-playbook/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Structures a VP or CPO's first 90 days as a **diagnostic process, not an execution sprint**. Core insight: premature action before building evidence causes costly reversals.

---

## Three-Phase Structure

| Phase | Objective | Key Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 — Diagnose | Build evidence base of actual organisational reality | Detailed reality notebook (uninterpreted) |
| Month 2 — Validate | Pattern-match, reality-check, identify people situations | Interpreted assessment with people map |
| Month 3 — Act with Evidence | Decisions grounded in evidence; introduce structure | Shared assessment, initial strategy, 3–5 active changes |

---

## Phase 0: Before Accepting

Ask the hiring CEO:
- Expectations for product in 90 days and one year?
- Who are the all-stars and why? Who has gaps and why?
- What constraints should you understand upfront?
- What does one-year success look like specifically?

**Red flags:** "You can't change the existing roadmap" / "Transform the organisation in six months" / misalignment between CEO's talent assessments and external feedback.

---

## People Assessment Framework

- **Diamonds in the rough** — capable, undervalued people lacking a champion
- **Strong people in wrong roles** — high performers mismatched to current scope; requires honest conversations

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Performing action instead of building evidence (making announcements to signal decisiveness before Month 3)
- Staying in consultant mode too long (still gathering data with no visible decisions by Month 3)
- Trusting loudest voices instead of building patterns across multiple sources
- Skipping Phase 0 interview before accepting the role
