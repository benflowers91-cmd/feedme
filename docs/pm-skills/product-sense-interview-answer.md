# Product-Sense Interview Answer

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/product-sense-interview-answer/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Structures responses to open-ended PM interview questions using a six-part framework. Prevents jumping to features without grounding in user needs, market context, and tradeoffs.

Use for product design, improvement, and "build next" interview questions. Not for STAR behavioural interviews, metrics-diagnosis cases, or go-to-market/pricing questions.

---

## Six-Part Answer Spine

1. **Clarify** — Reduce ambiguity and state assumptions (1–2 questions max — unblock, don't stall)
2. **Rationale** — Explain market timing and business fit
3. **Product Goal** — Define user outcome *before* features
4. **Segmentation** — Choose a specific target with clear reasoning (not just demographics — use dimensions that materially change product needs: expertise, stakes, workflow)
5. **Pain Points** — Map the journey, prioritise one friction by frequency and severity
6. **Solution** — Generate three genuinely distinct options, then commit to one MVP with explicit tradeoffs

---

## Key Delivery Principles

- Ask only 1–2 clarifying questions to unblock, not stall
- Conversational short sentences — not memo-style delivery
- Prioritise pain points by frequency and severity, not breadth
- Generate three distinct solutions *before* choosing
- Build segmentation on dimensions that change product needs, not demographics

---

## What This Is Not

Feature brainstorm / fake market sizing / rigid script / business-case-only thinking divorced from user behaviour.
