# Stakeholder Mapping

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/stakeholder-mapping/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Prioritises stakeholders and establishes engagement strategies using two complementary 2×2 grids. Run after `stakeholder-identification.md`.

---

## Grid 1: Power × Interest (Engagement Strategy)

| | High Interest | Low Interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High Power** | Manage closely | Keep satisfied |
| **Low Power** | Keep informed | Monitor |

---

## Grid 2: Impact × Power (Equity Check)

| | High Power | Low Power |
|---|---|---|
| **High Impact** | Manage closely (Q2) | Elevate voice deliberately (Q1) |
| **Low Impact** | Manage relationship lightly (Q4) | Monitor minimally (Q3) |

---

## Key Insight

A stakeholder who appears in "keep informed" on Grid 1 but "high impact, low power" on Grid 2 is someone you've been under-engaging — with consequences that fall entirely on them.

---

## Application Steps

1. **Assess independently** — run each grid without referencing the other
2. **Compare outputs** — identify misalignments between engagement strategy and actual impact
3. **Plan migration** — move Q1 stakeholders (high impact, low power) into active design and review roles
4. **Document actions** — assign ownership for each concrete engagement change

---

## Common Failure

Leaving Q1 stakeholders "decorative" on the canvas while maintaining existing engagement patterns. High-impact, low-power stakeholders need a *named research slot, a co-design session, a role in requirements review* — not just an entry on a grid.

Feed outputs into `stakeholder-engagement-advisor.md` for per-stakeholder tactical planning.
