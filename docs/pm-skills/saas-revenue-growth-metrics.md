## Purpose

Calculate and interpret key SaaS metrics for understanding business momentum and product-market fit. Pairs with `saas-economics-efficiency-metrics.md` — that skill covers unit economics; this covers revenue and retention.

---

## Core Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| MRR/ARR | Monthly/Annual recurring revenue | Track components: new, expansion, churn, contraction |
| ARPU/ARPA | Avg revenue per user/account | Use together — divergence signals monetisation gaps |
| ACV | Annual contract value | Enables comparison across contract structures |
| Revenue Quality | Gross vs. net revenue | Discounting and refunds appear here first |

---

## Critical Retention Metrics

**Churn Rate**
- <2%/month: healthy
- >5%/month: requires immediate intervention

**NRR (Net Revenue Retention)** = (Start MRR + Expansion - Churn - Contraction) / Start MRR
- >100%: growing without new customers (the holy grail)
- <90%: customers are leaving faster than you're expanding

**Quick Ratio** = (New + Expansion MRR) / (Churned + Contraction MRR)
- >4: strong growth sustainability

**Expansion Revenue** — upsells, cross-sells, usage growth from existing customers. The most efficient revenue you can generate.

---

## Key Analytical Frameworks

**Revenue Mix Analysis** — Which products actually fund the business? High concentration in one product is risk.

**Cohort Analysis** — Are newer customers performing better or worse than older ones? Cohort degradation is the "silent killer of SaaS" — the company looks healthy in aggregate while the underlying trend is worsening.

---

## Critical Warnings

- Never mix revenue growth with profitability — growing revenue with collapsing margins is the opposite of progress
- Annual churn compounds: 2%/month = 21%/year, not 24%
- Logo churn and revenue churn are different — losing 10 small customers while retaining 1 large one looks fine on revenue churn but signals a problem in logo churn
- **Blended metrics hide critical trends** — always segment before concluding
