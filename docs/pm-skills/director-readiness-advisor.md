# Director Readiness Advisor

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/director-readiness-advisor/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Coaching for the PM-to-Director transition. Diagnoses your specific situation (not generic leadership advice) and delivers targeted, experience-backed recommendations.

---

## Four Transition Situations

Choose the one that matches where you are:

1. **Preparing to make the leap** — still a PM, building toward Director
2. **Interviewing for Director roles** — active internal promotion or external search
3. **Newly landed** — recently promoted or hired (first 6 months)
4. **Recalibrating** — Director for a while; something isn't working

The advice differs significantly across these. Applying "newly landed" coaching to someone two years into the role misses the actual problem.

---

## Underlying Framework

Draws on the **Altitude & Horizon Framework** (see `altitude-horizon-framework.md`). Named failure modes:
- Hero Syndrome
- Allergic to Process
- People-Pleaser Leadership
- Instant Gratification Trap

---

## Session Structure

Choose entry mode:
1. **Guided** — one question at a time
2. **Context dump** — share your situation upfront
3. **Best guess** — high-value advice for the most common scenario (newly landed, 0–3 months)

---

## Common Pitfalls

- **Treating preparation like a checklist** — readiness isn't task completion
- **Misidentifying your situation** — be honest about where you actually are, not where you'd like to be
- **Looking for permission** — there's no graduation test; use this to identify what's hardest for you specifically
