# AI-Shaped Readiness Advisor

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/ai-shaped-readiness-advisor/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Assesses whether your product work is **AI-first** (efficiency gains — using AI to do existing things faster) or **AI-shaped** (organisational transformation — rethinking the product around what AI makes possible). These are fundamentally different bets requiring different strategy, resourcing, and risk tolerance.

---

## The Core Distinction

| | AI-First | AI-Shaped |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Efficiency gains in existing workflows | Reimagining the product and org around AI |
| Risk | Low — incremental improvement | High — requires new capabilities |
| Timeline | Weeks to months | Quarters to years |
| Success metric | Speed / cost reduction | New value created for customers |

---

## Assessment Process

13 questions total (8 context + 5 scoring). Choose your mode:

1. **Guided** — one question at a time
2. **Context dump** — share what you know, skip redundant questions
3. **Best guess** — provide basics, advisor makes reasonable assumptions

---

## Key Questions the Assessment Explores

- Where are you using AI today — in the product, in the process, or both?
- Are you optimising existing workflows or enabling workflows that didn't exist before?
- Does your org have the capabilities (data, ML, AI ops) to sustain what you're building?
- Are you solving customer problems or internal efficiency problems?

---

## Common Pitfall

Teams often call AI-first work "AI-shaped" because the vocabulary sounds more ambitious. The advisor helps you be honest about which one you're actually doing — and whether you're resourced for the bet you're making.
