# Customer Journey Map

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/customer-journey-map/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Visualises customer interactions across all stages — awareness through loyalty — while documenting actions, touchpoints, emotions, KPIs, business goals, and team ownership. Combines customer empathy with business metrics to drive actionable experience improvements.

Use one persona per map. Don't mix.

---

## Structure

**Five stages:** Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Service → Loyalty

**Six dimensions per stage:**
1. Customer Actions (observable behaviours)
2. Touchpoints (interaction channels)
3. Customer Experience (emotions and thoughts)
4. KPIs (measurable success indicators)
5. Business Goals (desired outcomes)
6. Teams Involved (cross-functional ownership)

---

## Prerequisites

Before mapping:
- Secure stakeholder participation (marketing, sales, product, support)
- Develop detailed personas with demographics and psychographics
- Define buying process stages
- Inventory all customer touchpoints (digital and physical)

---

## Common Failure Modes

- Generic emotions ("customer feels happy") — not actionable
- Missing offline touchpoints
- Mapping internal assumptions rather than actual behaviour
- Omitting KPIs — prevents measurement
- Treating maps as one-time exercises — they go stale

---

## This Is Not

Not a user story map (product workflows), service blueprint (internal processes), or user flow (specific interactions). Journey maps are strategic artifacts.
