# Product Strategy Session

**Type:** Workflow
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/product-strategy-session/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

A structured, multi-phase workflow (2–4 weeks) that moves from strategic ambiguity to validated, executable product direction. Orchestrates six interconnected phases.

**Not for:** feature brainstorming, waterfall planning, or solo PM work — requires cross-functional participation.

---

## Six Phases

| Phase | Focus | Key Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Positioning & Market Context | Target customer, problem space, differentiation | Positioning statement, proto-personas, JTBD map |
| 2. Problem Framing & Validation | Validate core customer problems | Problem framing canvas, formal problem statements |
| 3. Solution Exploration | Generate and prioritise solution options | Opportunity solution tree, PoL experiments |
| 4. Prioritisation & Roadmap | Rank and sequence initiatives | ICE/RICE-scored backlog, quarterly roadmap |
| 5. Stakeholder Alignment | Present and refine strategy | Stakeholder presentation, press-release exercise |
| 6. Execution Planning | Break top epics into stories | User stories, sprint plan |

---

## Decision Gates

After Phase 1: "Do we have enough customer context?"
After Phase 2: "Is the problem validated?"
After Phase 3: "Do we need to test solutions before committing?"

These gates adapt the timeline — low-uncertainty work moves faster.

---

## Time Investment

- Minimum: 2 weeks (no discovery or experiments)
- Typical: 3 weeks (one validation round)
- Maximum: 4–6 weeks (includes interviews and testing)

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Skipping problem validation and jumping to solutions
- Running without executive sponsorship
- Treating it as a permanent process rather than a 2–4 week sprint
