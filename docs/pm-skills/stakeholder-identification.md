# Stakeholder Identification

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/stakeholder-identification/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Map every stakeholder before engaging anyone. Creates an equity-aware, comprehensive stakeholder inventory before prioritisation or engagement planning begins. Completeness first — ranking comes downstream in `stakeholder-mapping.md`.

---

## Three Relationship Categories

- **Allies** — active supporters of the initiative
- **Audiences** — those impacted by outcomes (directly or indirectly)
- **Influencers** — those shaping decisions without direct participation

Stakeholders who appear in multiple categories are high-leverage relationships — handle with care.

---

## R/P/D Marking System

Tag each stakeholder with what they control:
- **R** — Resources (budget, people, data, access)
- **P** — Permission (approvals, clearance)
- **D** — Decision (final say on scope or launch)

---

## Equity Lens Questions

- Who bears the product's costs or risks without power to shape its design?
- Whose perspective is missing because we assumed someone else represents them?

---

## Six-Step Process

1. Brainstorm unconstrained — anyone plausibly connected, no filtering
2. Categorise into Allies / Audiences / Influencers
3. Apply R/P/D marking
4. Apply equity lens — identify secondary and tertiary effect stakeholders
5. Notice team biases — who was named or overlooked and why?
6. Narrow to 2–3 priority targets with "what we need to learn" statements

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating initial brainstorms as final lists
- Listing roles rather than specific people
- Skipping the bias-check step
- Running solo without validation
- Generating lists without attached next steps
