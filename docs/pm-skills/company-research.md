# Company Research

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/company-research/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Strategic intelligence gathering on a company for competitive analysis, partnership evaluation, interview prep, or market-entry decisions. Nine-step process that goes beyond websites into earnings calls, podcasts, and engineering blogs.

---

## Nine-Step Process

1. Define research scope — company, purpose, key questions
2. Gather company overview — basic info, history, industry context
3. Extract executive quotes — CEO, COO, VP Product on strategic vision
4. Document product insights — strategy, recent launches, product philosophy
5. Identify transformation strategies — digital, AI, Agile initiatives
6. Understand PM's organisational role — how PM influences strategy
7. Analyse future roadmap — upcoming initiatives, competitive challenges
8. Document PLG insights — product-led growth and data-driven decisions (if applicable)
9. Synthesise key takeaways — strategic principles and applicable lessons

---

## Best Sources

- Earnings call transcripts
- Podcast interviews and conference talks
- Executive blog posts and LinkedIn content
- Product blogs and changelogs
- Engineering team documentation
- Job postings (reveal team structure and priorities)

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Surface-level research without strategic depth
- Unverified claims lacking source attribution
- Mixing opinion with factual observation
- Relying on outdated information (prioritise last 12–24 months)
- Overlooking competitive threats and challenges

---

## What This Is Not

Not for financial analysis, internal strategic planning, SWOT assessments, or as a substitute for customer research.
