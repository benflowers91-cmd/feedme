# Roadmap Planning

**Type:** Workflow
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/roadmap-planning/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Transforms strategy into an executable roadmap by orchestrating prioritisation, epic definition, stakeholder alignment, and sequencing. Addresses the "feature factory" problem — roadmaps that lack strategic narrative and customer focus.

---

## Five Phases (1–2 Weeks Total)

**Phase 1: Gather Inputs**
- Business goals (OKRs), customer problems from discovery, technical constraints, stakeholder requests

**Phase 2: Define Initiatives**
- Convert inputs into 10–15 epics as hypothesis statements:
  "We believe building X for [persona] achieves [outcome] because [assumption]"
- Add success metrics and T-shirt effort estimates (S/M/L/XL)

**Phase 3: Prioritise Initiatives**
- Apply ICE or RICE to score epics
- Apply strategic adjustments for alignment with business bets

**Phase 4: Sequence Roadmap**
- Organise into quarters or Now/Next/Later buckets
- Explicitly map dependencies
- Validate feasibility with engineering

**Phase 5: Communicate Roadmap**
- 30–45 minute presentation emphasising strategic context, outcome focus, and flexibility
- Gather stakeholder feedback before publishing

---

## Roadmap Formats

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| Now/Next/Later | Agile teams, uncertain timelines |
| Theme-Based | Executive communication |
| Timeline | Resource planning |

**Anti-pattern:** Feature-based roadmaps without context. Nobody remembers a feature list; everyone remembers a story.

---

## Common Failures

- Feature-driven roadmaps without strategic narrative
- HiPPO-based prioritisation (Highest Paid Person's Opinion)
- Treating roadmaps as inflexible commitments
- Unmapped technical dependencies
- Solo PM creation without cross-functional input
