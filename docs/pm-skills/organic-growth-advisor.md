# Organic Growth Advisor

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/organic-growth-advisor/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Fast triage to identify which organic growth path to pursue when growth is stalling and direction is unclear. Based on McKinsey Growth Pyramid levels 2–5.

**Not a comprehensive strategy tool** — it's a triage to decide which lane to test first.

**When NOT to use:** Before achieving core product-market fit / mid-experiment / without basic customer-market context / when you need rigorous strategic planning.

---

## The Growth Path Matrix (2×2)

|  | Low Product Change | High Product Change |
|---|---|---|
| **Known Market Context** | **L2: New Customer Segments** — adjacent buyers with similar problems | **L4: New Distribution Channels** — same value prop, better access |
| **Less Known Market Context** | **L3: New Geographies** — product translates; market context doesn't automatically | **L5: New Products/Services** — adjacent jobs customers need |

---

## Key Principle

> "Innovation at any level is downstream of accumulated contextual knowledge."

Teams succeed by studying how value works in new markets before scaling, not by moving fastest.

---

## Common Pitfall

Teams often pursue L5 (most knowledge, capital, and time required) before exhausting L2 and L4 opportunities. This is the most common organic growth mistake.
