# PESTEL Analysis

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/pestel-analysis/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Systematic evaluation of macro-environmental factors for market entry decisions, multi-year strategic planning, and risk evaluation for new initiatives. Reveals external forces beyond your control for proactive rather than reactive planning.

**Use for:** Market entry decisions, geographic expansion, multi-year strategic planning, new initiative risk evaluation.
**Avoid for:** Short-term tactical decisions, direct competitor comparison, customer preference research, stable well-understood markets.

---

## Six Categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Political** | Government policies, regulatory stability, trade rules, tax frameworks |
| **Economic** | Growth metrics, inflation, currency fluctuations, spending patterns |
| **Social** | Demographic shifts, cultural movements, lifestyle evolution, attitudes |
| **Technological** | Innovation trends, automation adoption, digital shifts |
| **Environmental** | Climate impacts, sustainability demands, resource constraints |
| **Legal** | Regulatory compliance, IP protection, employment standards, safety requirements |

---

## Critical Success Factors

**Specificity matters.** "Regulations exist" creates no value. Connect each factor directly to your product: "EU AI Act requires transparent decision-making — our automation tool needs explainability features by Q3 2026."

**Include data sources.** Unattributed claims lack credibility.

**Acknowledge minimal-impact factors.** Not every category affects every product. If Environmental is low relevance, say so.

**Synthesise into action.** Analysis without recommendations wastes effort.

**Treat as recurring.** Review annually or when major external events occur.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Generic lists without product-specific implications
- Claims without citations
- Lengthy analysis that doesn't inform actual decisions
- Completing it once and abandoning it
