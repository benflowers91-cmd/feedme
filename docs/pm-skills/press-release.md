# Press Release (Working Backwards)

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/press-release/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Amazon's "Working Backwards" methodology — define customer value before building. Not a launch-day marketing artifact; a forcing function for clarity and stakeholder alignment.

> "If you can't write a compelling press release, the product idea may be weak."

Use when defining new products, securing stakeholder buy-in before development, or testing whether a concept resonates. Skip for trivial tweaks or post-launch polishing.

---

## Structure

1. **Headline** — Benefit-focused announcement (outcomes, not feature lists)
2. **Dateline & Introduction** — Context and key problem addressed
3. **Problem Statement** — Validated customer pain with supporting data
4. **Solution Description** — Outcomes achieved (not technical specs)
5. **Leadership Quote** — Visionary perspective emphasising customer commitment
6. **Supporting Details** — Additional validated benefits and metrics
7. **Company Boilerplate** — Organisational background
8. **Call to Action** — How customers learn more

---

## Critical Success Factors

- **Specificity:** "Reduces invoice processing from 8 hours to 10 minutes" vs. "streamlines workflows"
- **Customer voice:** Avoid technical terminology; explain outcomes in human terms
- **Data-driven:** Claims need supporting evidence — customer quotes, research, beta metrics
- **Measurable benefits:** Quantify time savings, cost reduction, or other concrete outcomes

---

## Common Failures

- Feature enumeration ("includes AI, ML, OCR") instead of outcomes
- Vague problem definitions ("solves inefficiency")
- Impenetrable corporate language
- Hollow executive statements ("We're excited to announce...")
