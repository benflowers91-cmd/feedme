# Business Health Diagnostic

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/business-health-diagnostic/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Evaluates SaaS business health across four integrated dimensions. Designed for board meetings, quarterly reviews, and fundraising preparation.

---

## Four Dimensions

1. **Growth & Retention** — Revenue expansion and customer persistence
2. **Unit Economics** — Customer-level profitability (CAC, LTV, payback)
3. **Capital Efficiency** — Cash utilisation and runway
4. **Strategic Position** — Competitive defensibility and market fit

---

## Stage-Specific Benchmarks

| Stage | Growth | NRR | Rule of 40 | Gross Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early (<$10M ARR) | >50% YoY | — | — | >70% |
| Growth ($10–50M ARR) | >40% YoY | >100% | >40 | — |
| Scale ($50M+ ARR) | >25% YoY | >110% | >40 | — |

---

## Red Flags — Immediate Action

- Runway under 6 months
- LTV:CAC below 1.5:1
- Accelerating monthly churn cohort-to-cohort
- NRR declining below 90%
- Magic Number under 0.3

---

## Diagnostic Patterns

| Pattern | Situation | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy | All dimensions meet stage benchmarks | Optimise |
| Moderate | Issues in 1–2 dimensions | Fix within 90 days |
| Concerning | Multiple critical flags | Immediate intervention on unit economics and retention |
| Critical | Existential risk | Drastic measures within days |

---

## Common Mistakes

- Isolating single metrics without systemic context
- Applying scale-stage expectations to early-stage companies
- Using only lagging indicators while ignoring leading signals
- Attempting to fix multiple problem areas simultaneously
