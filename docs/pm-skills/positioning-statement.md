# Positioning Statement

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/positioning-statement/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Craft a strategic positioning statement (Geoffrey Moore format) that clarifies your target market, customer need, product category, and competitive differentiation.

A positioning statement is a **strategic clarity tool**, not a tagline or marketing slogan.

---

## Template

```
VALUE PROPOSITION
For [target customer]
that need [underserved need]
[product name] is a [product category]
that [benefit statement — measurable outcome, not feature].

DIFFERENTIATION STATEMENT
Unlike [primary competitor or alternative customers actually consider]
[product name] provides [unique differentiation].

ONE-SENTENCE SUMMARY
[Combine into a single sentence for internal use]
```

---

## Quality Checks

1. **Target clarity** — Could you describe this customer to a recruiter specifically?
2. **Need validation** — Does this resonate emotionally, or is it generic?
3. **Competitor honesty** — Are you naming the *actual* alternative buyers consider?
4. **Differentiation substance** — Could a competitor replicate this in 6 months?
5. **Decision guidance** — Does this positioning help answer "should we build feature X?"

---

## Common Mistakes

- Positioning for everyone rather than a specific first segment
- Listing features as benefits instead of outcomes
- Naming straw-man competitors instead of real alternatives
- Making unsubstantiated claims ("revolutionary," "unmatched")
- Choosing unclear product categories

---

## Validate Externally

Test your positioning with 5 target customers to confirm market resonance before finalising.
