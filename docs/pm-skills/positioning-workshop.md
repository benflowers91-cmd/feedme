# Positioning Workshop

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/positioning-workshop/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Guided discovery to articulate product positioning through five sequential questions. Produces a Geoffrey Moore-style positioning statement with value proposition, differentiation, and one-sentence summary.

Use when you need to develop positioning from scratch, or when existing positioning feels vague and isn't guiding decisions.

---

## Five Discovery Questions

1. **Target Customer Segment** — B2B SMB / enterprise / mass-market / niche?
2. **Underserved Need** — What pain point, through a Jobs-to-be-Done lens?
3. **Product Category** — How will buyers evaluate this solution?
4. **Key Benefit** — Measurable outcome, not features ("reduces manual work from 10 hours/week to 1 hour")
5. **Competitive Differentiation** — What makes this defensible versus the primary alternative?

---

## Output

- Geoffrey Moore positioning statement (value proposition + differentiation)
- One-sentence positioning summary for internal alignment

---

## Critical Success Factors

- Specificity over vagueness — "reduces manual work from 10 hours/week to 1 hour" vs. "improves productivity"
- Feature-based differentiation is weak — features can be copied; capabilities, data, and network effects can't
- Measurable benefits only — unmeasurable benefits fail customer validation

---

## Validation Step

Test with 5 target customers before finalising. Positioning that doesn't resonate with real customers isn't positioning — it's aspiration.
