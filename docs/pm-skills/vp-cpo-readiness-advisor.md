## Purpose

Coaching for the Director-to-VP or VP-to-CPO transition. Diagnoses your specific situation and delivers targeted, experience-based recommendations — not generic leadership advice.

Pairs with `director-readiness-advisor.md` (for the PM-to-Director step) and `executive-onboarding-playbook.md` (for the first 90 days once you've landed the role).

---

## The Three Ps Framework

Executive product leadership expands across three dimensions simultaneously:

- **Product** — Portfolio strategy and roadmap coherence
- **Practice** — Execution discipline and cross-functional rhythms
- **People** — Organisational structure, talent development, leadership systems

Most Directors excel at Product, manage Practice adequately. The People dimension is where transitions typically break down.

---

## The Core Insight: Empowerment Myth

> "A PM's Rubik's Cube is 3×3. A Director's is 5×5. A VP's is 7×7. A CPO's is 9×9."

Promotion doesn't grant freedom to execute your vision — constraints evolve, they don't disappear. Success requires building capacity to navigate larger constraints.

---

## VP → CPO Paradigm Shift

The most significant cognitive reframe in the product leadership track:

| | VP Mindset | CPO Mindset |
|---|---|---|
| Primary focus | Product portfolio performance | Business outcomes the product org drives |
| Language | Product terminology | Business and financial terminology |
| Primary customer | Your product org | Your executive team |

---

## Four Transition Situations

1. Preparing to make the leap — still a Director, building toward VP
2. Evaluating or interviewing — active search or specific opportunity
3. Newly landed — first 6 months in VP/CPO role
4. Recalibrating — already in role; something isn't working

---

## Common Pitfall

Alliance building is non-optional at this level. Weekly engagement with peer executives, proactive communication about trade-offs, and bringing stakeholders along before decisions are announced — this determines whether you can operate effectively.
