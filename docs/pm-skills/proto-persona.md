# Proto-Persona

**Type:** Component
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/proto-persona/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

A lightweight user hypothesis synthesised from available research, market signals, and team knowledge. Created in hours/days (not weeks/months). Structured placeholder that evolves rapidly as you learn.

Start with one primary persona. Focus, then expand.

---

## Six-Step Framework

1. **Gather Context** — Collect existing user research, analytics, market data, stakeholder insights. Document gaps rather than fabricating missing pieces.
2. **Define Identity** — Memorable name + behavioural demographics (how they actually work and communicate, not just age and location)
3. **Capture Voice** — Authentic quotes revealing mindset and frustrations. Real quotes from interviews or support tickets carry more weight than invented ones.
4. **Document Context** — Specific pains, observable accomplishments, tactical and aspirational goals
5. **Understand Influences** — Decision-making authority, who influences them, relevant beliefs shaping adoption
6. **Validate and Iterate** — Share with teams, tag assumptions, evolve as you learn

---

## Template

```
NAME: [Memorable, often alliterative]
ROLE & CONTEXT: [Specific role + how they work]
QUOTE: "[Authentic voice — real or placeholder]" [ASSUMPTION — VALIDATE]
PAINS: [Specific obstacles]
GOALS: [Tactical + aspirational]
INFLUENCES: [Who/what shapes their decisions]
OPEN ASSUMPTIONS: [What needs validation]
```

---

## Critical Anti-Patterns

- **Demographics without behaviour** — age and location don't predict adoption
- **Treating assumptions as facts** — tag everything uncertain with `[ASSUMPTION — VALIDATE]`
- **Creating too many personas** — start with one primary
- **Fabricating quotes** — missing quotes should be placeholders, not invented
- **Never validating** — proto-personas become liabilities if left unchanged for months
