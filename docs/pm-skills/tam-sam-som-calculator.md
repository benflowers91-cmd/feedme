# TAM/SAM/SOM Calculator

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/tam-sam-som-calculator/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Builds defensible market size estimates backed by real-world citations, economic projections, and population data. Use for investor pitches, budget decisions, and product validation.

---

## Three-Tier Framework

**TAM (Total Addressable Market)** — Total market demand at 100% capture

**SAM (Serviceable Available Market)** — Realistically targetable segment (geography, firmographics, product fit)

**SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)** — Portion capturable in 1–3 years (accounts for competition, GTM capacity)

---

## Four-Question Process

1. **Problem Space** — B2B SaaS / fintech / healthcare / e-commerce / other?
2. **Geographic Region** — US / EU / global / specific country?
3. **Industry/Market Segments** — narrows focus with cited population or revenue data
4. **Customer Demographics/Firmographics** — identifies addressable population

---

## Output

A markdown report with:
- Population estimates with citations
- Market size calculations showing the maths
- Year 1–3 revenue projections
- Competition and GTM assumptions
- Data source references

---

## Common Pitfalls

- TAM claims without citations — unattributed market sizes are fiction
- SOM inflated to equal SAM — accounts for no competitive pressure
- Missing customer population counts — "the market is $10B" without specifying who
- Static assumptions — update when entering new markets or geographies
- Ignoring sales capacity constraints — SOM is bounded by what you can actually sell

---

## When to Use

Investor pitches, product prioritisation, growth target-setting, market validation.

**When NOT to use:** Internal tools with captive users / before problem definition / as sole validation method.
