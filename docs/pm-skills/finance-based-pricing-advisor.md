# Finance-Based Pricing Advisor

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/finance-based-pricing-advisor/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Evaluates the financial impact of pricing changes using ARPU analysis, conversion metrics, churn risk, NRR effects, and CAC payback implications. Outputs a go/no-go recommendation.

**Covers:** Price increases, new tiers, add-ons, discounts, packaging changes, usage-based pricing.

**Does NOT cover:** Value-based pricing frameworks, willingness-to-pay research, competitive positioning, psychological pricing, packaging architecture, or monetisation model selection — those are separate skills.

---

## Five-Step Process

1. Gather context — current ARPU, churn rates, conversion data, CAC
2. Identify pricing change type
3. Estimate expected impact — revenue, conversion, churn, expansion effects
4. Evaluate current state — baseline metrics and competitive position
5. Deliver recommendation or test strategy

---

## Recommendation Patterns

| Net Revenue Impact | Churn Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|
| >10% ARPU lift | Minimal | Implement broadly |
| Uncertain | Moderate | Test first |
| Positive revenue | Significant churn risk | Modify approach |
| Churn losses > price gains | Any | Don't change |

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Ignoring churn impact of price increases
- Failing to grandfather existing customers
- Testing without statistical power
- Omitting value justification for price increases
