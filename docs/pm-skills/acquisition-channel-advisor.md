# Acquisition Channel Advisor

**Type:** Interactive
**Source:** [deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/blob/main/skills/acquisition-channel-advisor/SKILL.md)

---

## Purpose

Evaluate whether to **scale, test, or kill** an acquisition channel using a structured financial framework. Uses unit economics, customer quality, scalability, and strategic fit to produce a data-backed recommendation.

**When NOT to use:** Channels <3 months old, <100 customers, or without channel-level data.

---

## Four Evaluation Pillars

1. **Unit Economics** — CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio, payback period
2. **Customer Quality** — Retention, churn, NRR, ICP fit by channel
3. **Scalability** — Magic Number, addressable volume, CAC trends
4. **Strategic Fit** — Alignment with GTM strategy and target segments

---

## Decision Logic

| LTV:CAC | Payback | Quality | Volume | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| >3:1 | <12 months | Good | High | Scale aggressively |
| 2–3:1 | 12–18 months | Average | Medium | Test & optimise |
| <2:1 | >18 months | Poor | Low | Kill or fix |

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Scaling channels with broken unit economics
- Optimising CAC while LTV collapses (the channel looks cheaper but customers churn faster)
- Celebrating vanity metrics — signups ≠ paid customers
- Ignoring payback period: 6:1 LTV:CAC with 48-month payback is a cash trap
